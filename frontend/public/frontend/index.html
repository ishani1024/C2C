How it works

From assessment to opportunity in four steps

A simple, repeatable path that keeps students moving toward a real career outcome.

  1. 1

    Assess

    Take structured technical and soft-skill assessments.

  2. 2

    Map

    Skills are mapped to career paths and industry roles.

  3. 3

    Learn

    Get recommended learning to close the identified gaps.

  4. 4

    Connect

    Apply to matched internships and jobs, and track progress.

Assess your skills→ Identify skill gaps→ Get recommendations→ Apply to opportunities
